### Leveraging General-Purpose AI Tools for Use in Specialized Tasks: Verifying User-Inputted Text 

#### Overview
The goal of this Capstone is to show how general-purpose AI tools can be utilized in specialized tasks. 

This project was made in Python and implements Google Gemini API. I tasked Gemini with analyzing the text inputted by the user and performing a deep-web search to verify its correctness, and reporting those results to the user. 

#### General-Purpose AI vs. Specialized AI
General-purpose AI tools are AI not trained for use in a specific task. Some examples of general-purpose AI can include ChatGPT, Claude, and CoPilot. 

Specialized-task AI is heavily trained for specific tasks.  

#### What benefits the potential benefits in using general-purpose AI in specialized tasks?
Compared to many specialized AI tools, general-purpose AI stools are significantly more accessible and are also incredibly easy to use.
